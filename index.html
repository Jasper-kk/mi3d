ABOUT THE DATASET

Lighting is the variable.
Not the scene.

A major challenge in collecting 3D data is capturing it under uncontrolled multi-illuminance in photon-limited scenes. Previous methods employed expensive LiDAR to mitigate illumination effects, often at the expense of real-world fidelity and model generalization.

MI3D captures point clouds across three previously underexplored illuminance levels using a dedicated Lighting Control System (LCS). Each scene is captured under all three lighting conditions and at four height levels: 1 m, 1.5 m, 2 m, and 2.3 m.

The dataset contains 45,723 paired RGB-D and point-cloud samples from 54 scenes and 92 distinct objects, supporting systematic evaluation of classification and segmentation under changing illumination.
